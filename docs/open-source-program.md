# Veqsio for Open Source

Open-source projects get the paid Veqsio features at no charge. If you maintain an open-source project, whether you are a company, a foundation or a group of volunteers, you can run Veqsio, including the Enterprise modules, for that project for free.

## Who qualifies

- The source of the project is published under an [OSI-approved licence](https://opensource.org/licenses) in a public repository.
- The project is alive: at least one release or commit in the last 12 months, or it is hosted by a recognised open-source foundation.
- The licence is used for the project's own work: tracking issues, planning and releases, community support, documentation and the people who contribute to it. Companies that publish open-source projects qualify for these projects. The licence does not cover the closed commercial products of a company or work for its customers.

## What you get

- A licence key for a self-hosted Veqsio with **every paid feature**: the Team and Enterprise features (SAML, OpenID Connect, SCIM, LDAP, issue security levels, audit export) and the modules Timesheets, Plans, ITSM, and Assets and CMDB.
- **Unlimited users**, valid for **12 months**, renewed by confirming that the project is still active.
- The same releases and updates as paying customers.

## What is not included

- Veqsio Cloud (our hosting). Cloud is charged at the normal price.
- Priority support and service levels. Support for the programme is by e-mail on a best-effort basis; the response targets on the [service levels](https://veqsio.com/sla) page do not apply.
- Custom development and migration work.

## Conditions

- Use the licence only for the qualifying project. Do not resell it, host Veqsio for others with it or pass the key on.
- The information you give us must be true. We may look at the public repository to check that the project exists and is active.
- Tell us when the project stops being open source or is abandoned.
- This is a licence of Workizon Ltd under the Veqsio Enterprise License (`ee/LICENSE`): it is a subscription at no charge, not a different licence. It is for your organisation and cannot be transferred.
- If the conditions are broken we may withdraw the licence after 30 days' notice, and immediately for serious abuse.
- When a licence ends, paid features switch off and nothing is deleted: your data stays readable and exportable.
- Mentioning that you use Veqsio is welcome and never required. We list a project publicly only with its permission.

## How to apply

Write to us through the [contact form](https://veqsio.com/#contact) on veqsio.com with the topic **Open source programme**. Tell us the name and address of the project, its licence, who maintains it, how many people would use Veqsio and what for. We reply personally. We keep your contact details and the key details for as long as the licence runs and as our records require; the form is used only to answer you.

## Changes

We may change the programme or stop accepting new projects. Licences that were issued stay valid until their end date.
