The Veqsio source code is free and open (AGPL-3.0). The **name "Veqsio" and the Veqsio logo are trademarks** of the
project and are *not* licensed under the AGPL. This policy explains what you may do with them. It exists so that
users can always tell the official product from other things built on its code.

## You may, without asking

- State truthfully that your product works with, integrates with or is compatible with Veqsio ("a plugin for
  Veqsio", "imports from Jira into Veqsio"), using the name in plain text.
- Write articles, tutorials, reviews and talks about Veqsio.
- Use the name to say that you run an **unmodified** official release.
- Fork the code under the AGPL. A fork is welcome, but see below.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use "Veqsio" or the logo as, or as part of, the name of a **modified version, fork, distribution or hosted
  service** ("Veqsio Cloud", "Veqsio Pro", "VeqsioHost"), or in a domain name or social media handle.
- Use the logo in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership or that your product is the official one.
- Alter the logo, or combine it with other marks.

## If you fork

Please choose a different name and logo, and remove the Veqsio logo (the files in `docs/assets/`, `packages/web/public/brand/`,
`packages/web/public/icons/`, `packages/web/app/icon.png`, the `Logo` component) before you distribute or host your version. You do not need to
remove the text "based on Veqsio" in your documentation.
