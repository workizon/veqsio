## 2. Commercial licence

A commercial licence is for organisations that:

- cannot or do not want to comply with the AGPL (for example, you want to embed Veqsio in a closed-source product or
  offer a modified hosted version without publishing your changes), or
- want the **Enterprise features and paid modules** (single sign-on with SAML, SCIM, LDAP, issue security, audit
  export and retention, ITSM, Assets/CMDB, Plans and Timesheets modules and similar), or
- want a support agreement with response-time commitments.

Paid modules are not part of this repository. They are delivered to licence holders, and the features are unlocked
with an offline licence key. Your data is never held hostage: if a licence ends, paid features switch off and
everything you stored stays readable and exportable.
